AI that actually writes well

Not just grammar fixes. ResuPal rewrites your bullets with specific language, varied structure, and the kind of phrasing that makes recruiters stop scrolling. No buzzwords. No templates. Just clear professional writing that sounds like a real person.

ATS optimizer

Most resumes never reach a human. ResuPal matches your resume to the job description and inserts the keywords hiring software looks for — without making it sound like a keyword list.

Cover letter generator

One click turns your resume into a tailored cover letter for any role. Specific, not generic. Adjusts the tone and emphasis based on the job description you paste in.

Job match score

Paste a job description and see exactly how well your resume matches — which keywords you have, which you are missing, and what to add before you apply.

Application tracker

Track every job you apply to in one place. Log company, role, status, date, and notes. See your pipeline at a glance — Applied, Interview, Offer, Rejected — and never lose track of where you stand.

Interview question prep

Based on your actual resume and target role, ResuPal generates the most likely interview questions you will face — behavioral, situational, and technical — with guidance on what each answer should focus on.

Skills gap analysis

Paste any job description and see exactly which skills you have, which are missing, and how to close each gap before you apply. Know what to learn, what to add, and what to deprioritize.

LinkedIn profile optimizer

Generate a polished LinkedIn About section from your resume in one click. Written in first person, no buzzwords, sounds like a real professional — not a copy-paste from your resume.

Salary range estimator

Get a realistic salary range based on your actual experience, skills, and location. Includes what is holding you to the low end, what would push you to the high end, and a negotiation tip before your next offer.

Live resume quality score

A score updates in real time as you type. Checks for key sections, quantified results, bullet structure, contact info, and flags weak language. Know exactly how strong your resume is before you send it.

Resume completeness checker

ResuPal scans your resume and tells you exactly what is missing — no summary, no quantified results, weak education — with specific suggestions to fix each gap and a priority list of what to do first.

Multiple resumes, one place

Tailor a different resume for every application. Duplicate any resume in one click, edit it for a specific role, and manage them all from one dashboard. Free plan includes 3, Pro gives you unlimited.

Cover letter + PDF download

Export your resume as a clean plain text file for ATS systems or a formatted PDF ready to attach to any application. Download your cover letter the same way. No extra steps.

Direct editing — always in control

The AI assists but you are always in control. Type directly in the editor, insert section headers with one click, apply only the AI suggestions you want, and use keyboard shortcuts to move fast. Your resume, your words — AI just makes them better.

FAQ

Good questions.

Does the AI make up experience I don't have? ↓
No. ResuPal only improves how your real experience is expressed. It never invents jobs, skills, or achievements. If your resume is vague, it asks you a specific question to get the real details before rewriting.
What makes Pro worth it over the free plan? ↓
The free plan gives you 3 AI actions per day and 3 saved resumes — enough to try it and fix one resume. Pro removes all limits: unlimited AI actions, unlimited saved resumes, ATS optimizer, and job tailoring. If you're actively job hunting, Pro pays for itself with the first application.
Will it work for my industry? ↓
Yes. ResuPal works for any role — tech, finance, healthcare, marketing, operations, creative, and more. The AI understands industry language and tailors its suggestions accordingly. You can also describe your target role and it adjusts.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime? ↓
Yes, cancel anytime from your Stripe billing portal. No questions, no penalties. Your account drops back to the free plan at the end of your billing period.
Is my resume data private? ↓
Your resume content is stored securely in your personal account — only you can see it. We don't sell your data or use it to train AI models. Each user's data is protected with row-level security so no one else can access it.
